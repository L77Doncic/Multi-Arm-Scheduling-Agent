图6: 异常处理决策流程 (harness_design.md)
================================================================================
graph TD
FAIL["执行失败"] --> EH["ExceptionHandler.handle()"]
EH --> CLASSIFY["异常分类"]
CLASSIFY --> CHECK{"重试次数 < 上限?"}
CHECK -->|是| RETRY["RETRY: 重新执行<br/>speed ↓15%, force ↑10%"]
CHECK -->|否| ESCALATE["策略升级"]
ESCALATE --> RECOVERY{"原策略类型"}
RECOVERY -->|RETRY| REPLAN["REPLAN: 返回资源分配"]
RECOVERY -->|FALLBACK| FB["FALLBACK: 切换仿真后端"]
RECOVERY -->|SKIP| SKIP["SKIP: 标记为 CANCELLED"]
style EH fill:#f3e5f5
style RETRY fill:#e8f5e9
style REPLAN fill:#fff3e0
style SKIP fill:#e1f5fe
